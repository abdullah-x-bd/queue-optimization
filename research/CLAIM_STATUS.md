## Claims
1. The uniform row-family optimal-clearance formula and active-counter theorem are accompanied by mathematical proofs in the note.
2. Minimum optional-portal results are exact for specific stated regimes, and bounded elsewhere.
3. The generic time-indexed MILP formulation is exact over a **fixed finite candidate design space** when the integer solver proves the required statuses.
4. Feasible MILP schedules are independently replayed; solver infeasibility statuses are numerical, not separately verified rational proof certificates.
5. The stochastic comparison enumerates the specified local policies and duration distribution; it is not a theorem of optimal stochastic control.
6. Novelty/priority in the entire operations-research literature is not established. No unrestricted optimal real-world floorplan is claimed.
